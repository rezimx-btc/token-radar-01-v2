The value model — fitted, not asserted

Launch value. launch = exp(6.964567 + 0.522266 × ln TVL) × growth × maturity × exp(category adjustment). The curve is an OLS fit of log market cap on log TVL over the 128 traded-token protocols in this snapshot that carry a market cap. Growth is 1 + clamp(change_7d, −60, 120) / 100 × 0.5; maturity is 0.6 + min(age, 1095) / 1095 × 0.4 (unknown age counts as 365 days). The category adjustment is the shrunk (n/(n+5)) median log-residual per category, capped at ±0.70 — Lending −0.37, Dexs +0.18, RWA +0.35, Risk Curators +0.33, Liquid Staking −0.59, Basis Trading +0.46. No protocol name is ever special-cased: the same function scores every row.

Launch odds. P(a protocol ever records a token), from the peers' own record: the category token rate, shrunk (k=20) toward the global 0.2171, times the age-band rate over the global rate, capped to [0.02, 0.60]. Old protocols record tokens more often than young ones (age ≥ 3y: 0.328; age < 6mo: 0.080). Yield (0.294) and Basis Trading (0.301) launch most by category; Risk Curators least (0.108).

Implied value = launch value × launch odds — the expected value of the launch. The board ranks by it.

Farm verdict. Farmers are assumed to share 5% of the expected value (a stated assumption, illustrative), split over an estimated farmer base of clamp(TVL / $25,000, 200, 50,000). Net per farmer is that share minus the costed plan (setup gas + weekly gas + 3% opportunity on the deposit). FARM when net > $50, THIN when net > $0, else SKIP. Every farm plan states its cost and its time.

Held-out validation — measured by the program on every run

Each test refits the curve on the train set only and predicts the test protocols' real market caps. The metric is the median absolute log error. The starter's flat 0.25 × TVL is the baseline in every test.

Integrity strip — what this board does not claim

  • “Records no token” is not “has no token.” The snapshot is DefiLlama's record; a protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry. Nothing here is proof of a future launch.
  • Wrapped, staked and bridged products are out. WBTC is Bitcoin on Ethereum and Binance staked ETH is ETH in a staking product — neither is a protocol waiting on a launch of its own. The board excludes the bridge, liquid-staking and CEX categories, wrapped/staked names, exchange-prefixed names and versioned pools, by the field guide's own rule.
  • The model is noisy. TVL explains only part of market cap (log-log R² 0.19 on the 128 traded protocols); the fitted median log error is about 1.2, so a typical launch value is right within roughly a factor of 3. Treat the ranking as a priority order, not a price target — and the median residual (+0.31 log points) means the curve is conservative on the median traded protocol.
  • Farm shares are an assumption. The 5% farmer pool and the TVL-based farmer count are sizing assumptions, stated so a reader can redo the verdict with their own numbers. Never pay to claim: a real airdrop never asks for a fee.
  • No network, no names. This page renders with no network from the snapshot alone, and no protocol is hand-picked: the same fitted function scores all 197 ranked rows.
